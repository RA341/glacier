Glacier is a self-hostable game library server that gives you full control over your collection. Download your ISOs from
your favorite sources and organize them into a clean, beautiful, and searchable library — complete with metadata,
artwork, and structured organization.

With the [Frost client](./install/frost), you can quickly download, install, and launch your collection from anywhere.
Fast syncing, streamlined downloads, and a simple interface make managing your collection effortless.

### Built for Control

* Self-hosted: your server, your rules
* No vendor lock-in
* Web UI for easy management

### Built for Sharing

* Share your library with friends
* Role-based access control

### Built to Scale

* Lightweight and efficient
    * at idle ram consumption is `<40mb`
* Designed for homelabs
* Works great with external storage

Whether you’re running a small personal server or building a shared game hub for friends, Glacier gives you the tools to
stay organized and in control.

**Glacier is the coolest way to store and manage your games.**
